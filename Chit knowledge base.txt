SECTION: GENERAL INFORMATION

Q: What is a Chit Fund?
A: A chit fund is a savings and borrowing scheme where a group of members contribute a fixed amount every month into a common fund. Each month, one member receives the pooled amount through an auction process.

Q: How does a Chit Fund work?
A: Members contribute monthly installments. The collected amount forms the chit value. An auction is conducted every cycle, and one member receives the prize amount. The process continues until all members receive the benefit.

Q: What is a Chit Cycle?
A: A chit cycle is one installment period, usually one month. The number of cycles generally equals the number of members in the chit group.

Q: Who can join a Chit Fund?
A: Any eligible individual who completes registration, submits required documents, and agrees to the terms and conditions can join.

Q: What are the benefits of joining a Chit Fund?
A: Chit funds help members save regularly and provide access to funds when needed through the auction process.

SECTION: MEMBERSHIP AND REGISTRATION
==================================================

Q: What documents are required to join a Chit Fund?
A: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Address Proof, Passport-size Photograph, and Bank Account Details.

Q: What is KYC?
A: KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is a process used to verify a subscriber's identity and address.

Q: Why is KYC mandatory?
A: KYC helps prevent fraud, ensures regulatory compliance, and protects both the subscriber and the company.

Q: How do I know if my KYC is verified?
A: You can check your KYC status through the application or by contacting your branch office.

Q: Can I update my registration details?
A: Yes. Registration details can be updated after verification and approval.

SECTION: INSTALLMENTS
==================================================

Q: What is an Installment?
A: An installment is the fixed monthly amount paid by every subscriber into the chit fund.

Q: How often should installments be paid?
A: Installments are generally paid once every month according to the chit schedule.

Q: What happens if I miss an installment?
A: Missing an installment may result in penalties, restrictions on auction participation, or delays in services.

Q: Can I pay installments online?
A: Yes. Installments can be paid using approved online payment methods.

Q: How can I check my installment history?
A: Installment history can be viewed in the passbook or account statement section.

Q: Is there a due date for installments?
A: Yes. Every cycle has a specified due date for installment payment.

==================================================
SECTION: AUCTIONS
==================================================

Q: What is a Chit Auction?
A: A chit auction is the process through which members bid for the prize amount available in a cycle.

Q: How does bidding work?
A: Members offer discounts on the chit value. The eligible bid selected according to company rules wins the auction.

Q: When are auctions conducted?
A: Auctions are generally conducted once during every chit cycle according to the announced schedule.

Q: How can I participate in an auction?
A: Eligible members with up-to-date payments can participate according to company policies.

Q: How can I check upcoming auction dates?
A: Upcoming auction schedules can be viewed in the application or obtained from the branch office.

Q: How do I know if I won an auction?
A: Auction results are displayed in the application and communicated through official notifications.

==================================================
SECTION: PRIZE AMOUNT
==================================================

Q: What is the Prize Amount?
A: The prize amount is the amount received by the successful bidder after applicable deductions.

Q: When will the prize amount be released?
A: Prize amounts are released after verification, surety approval, and completion of required procedures.

Q: Can prize money be delayed?
A: Yes. Delays may occur if verification or surety requirements are incomplete.

Q: Do I need documents before receiving prize money?
A: Yes. Required documents and sureties must be approved before disbursement.

==================================================
SECTION: DIVIDEND
==================================================

Q: What is a Dividend?
A: Dividend is the share of the discount amount distributed among subscribers after deducting the foreman's commission.

Q: How is Dividend calculated?
A: Dividend is calculated from the auction discount amount after applicable deductions and distributed among eligible subscribers.

Q: How do I receive Dividend?
A: Dividend is generally adjusted against future installments or handled according to company policy.

Q: Can I view my Dividend history?
A: Yes. Dividend details can be viewed through the passbook or account summary.

Q: Does every member receive a Dividend?
A: Eligible subscribers may receive dividends according to chit rules and auction results.

==================================================
SECTION: SUBSCRIBER TYPES
==================================================

Q: What is a Prized Subscriber (PS)?
A: A Prized Subscriber is a member who has already received the prize amount.

Q: What is a Non-Prized Subscriber (NPS)?
A: A Non-Prized Subscriber is a member who has not yet received the prize amount.

Q: Can a Prized Subscriber stop paying installments?
A: No. Prized subscribers must continue paying installments until completion of the chit.

SECTION: SURETY AND SECURITY
==================================================

Q: What is a Surety?
A: A surety is a person or asset that provides security and guarantees obligations related to prize disbursement.

Q: Why is Surety required?
A: Surety helps protect the company and subscribers by ensuring compliance with repayment obligations.

Q: What types of Surety are accepted?
A: Personal guarantors, property documents, fixed deposits, and other approved securities.

Q: How long does Surety verification take?
A: Verification depends on document completeness and approval processes.

Q: Can prize money be released without Surety?
A: Prize release requirements are governed by company policy and applicable regulations.
