==================================================
GENERAL INSTAGRAM FAQ
==================================================

Q: What is Instagram?
A: Instagram is a social media platform that allows users to share photos, videos, stories, and reels with their followers.

Q: Who owns Instagram?
A: Instagram is owned by Meta Platforms, the company that also owns Facebook and WhatsApp.

Q: What is an Instagram account?
A: An Instagram account is a user profile that allows a person to post content, follow other users, and interact on the platform.

Q: What is an Instagram profile?
A: An Instagram profile is a user's personal page containing their photo, bio, posts, followers, and following information.

Q: What is a follower?
A: A follower is a user who subscribes to another user's content and updates.

Q: What is following?
A: Following refers to the accounts that a user has chosen to follow.

Q: What is an Instagram post?
A: A post is a photo, video, or carousel shared permanently on a user's profile.

Q: What is an Instagram Story?
A: A Story is a photo or video that disappears automatically after 24 hours.

Q: What is an Instagram Reel?
A: A Reel is a short-form video designed for entertainment, creativity, and engagement.

Q: What is Instagram Live?
A: Instagram Live allows users to broadcast live video to their followers in real time.

Q: What is a hashtag?
A: A hashtag is a keyword preceded by the # symbol used to categorize content and improve discoverability.

Q: What is the Explore page?
A: The Explore page shows personalized content recommendations based on a user's interests and activity.

Q: What is a Direct Message (DM)?
A: A Direct Message is a private conversation between Instagram users.

Q: What is a private account?
A: A private account restricts content visibility to approved followers only.

Q: What is a public account?
A: A public account allows anyone to view content and interact with the profile.

Q: What is a business account?
A: A business account provides analytics, promotion tools, and contact options for organizations.

Q: What is a creator account?
A: A creator account is designed for influencers and content creators with advanced insights and audience tracking.

Q: What are Instagram Insights?
A: Instagram Insights provide analytics about reach, impressions, engagement, and audience activity.

Q: How can users increase engagement?
A: Users can post consistently, create quality content, use relevant hashtags, and interact with followers.

INSTAGRAM TROUBLESHOOTING FAQ
==================================================

Q: Why can't I log in to Instagram?
A: Possible reasons include incorrect credentials, internet connectivity problems, account restrictions, or server issues.

Q: What should I do if I forget my password?
A: Use the "Forgot Password" option and follow the password reset instructions sent to your email or phone.

Q: Why is Instagram not loading?
A: Check your internet connection, restart the application, clear cache, or update the app.

Q: Why are stories or posts not appearing?
A: This may occur due to poor internet connectivity, app glitches, or temporary server issues.

Q: Why can't I upload photos or videos?
A: Ensure you have sufficient storage, stable internet, appropriate permissions, and the latest app version.

Q: Why are my messages not sending?
A: Message failures can occur because of network issues, temporary restrictions, or Instagram service interruptions.

Q: Why is my account temporarily locked?
A: Instagram may lock an account after suspicious activity, repeated failed logins, or policy violations.

Q: Why am I not receiving a password reset email?
A: Verify your email address and check spam or junk folders.

Q: Why can't I follow new accounts?
A: Instagram may temporarily limit account actions when unusual activity or rate limits are detected.

Q: Why are my reels getting low views?
A: Low views can result from audience engagement levels, content quality, posting frequency, or algorithm recommendations.

Q: What should I do if my account is hacked?
A: Change your password immediately, review login activity, enable two-factor authentication, and report the compromise.

LOGOUT & SESSION MANAGEMENT
==================================================

Q: How do I log out of Instagram?
A: Go to your profile, open Settings and Privacy, and select Log Out.

Q: What happens when I log out?
A: The active user session is removed from the device and authentication is required to log in again.

Q: How can I view login activity?
A: Open Settings → Security → Login Activity to see active and recent sessions.

Q: How can I remove an unknown device?
A: Review Login Activity and choose Log Out for any unrecognized session.

Q: Can I log out from all devices?
A: Changing your password typically signs out most active sessions and requires reauthentication.

Q: How can I secure my account after using a public device?
A: Log out, change your password, and enable two-factor authentication.

==================================================
LOGOUT BLOCKING & SECURITY FAQ
==================================================

Q: What is logout blocking?
A: Logout blocking is a security mechanism that prevents unauthorized or unsafe logout actions under specific conditions.

Q: Why might a logout request be blocked?
A: The system may block logout requests during security checks, ongoing transactions, or suspicious activity investigations.

Q: What happens if logout fails?
A: The system should display an error message and allow the user to retry the operation.

Q: What is session timeout?
A: Session timeout automatically ends a user session after a period of inactivity.

Q: Why am I automatically logged out?
A: Automatic logout may happen because of inactivity, password changes, security updates, or account protection measures.

Q: How can suspicious logout activities be detected?
A: Systems can monitor abnormal logout attempts, repeated session terminations, unusual IP addresses, and device changes.

Q: What security features help protect user sessions?
A: Session invalidation, token revocation, multi-factor authentication, device verification, and login monitoring.

Q: Can administrators restrict logout?
A: Some enterprise systems can restrict logout during maintenance windows, audits, or critical workflows.

Q: How can a chatbot help with logout issues?
A: A chatbot can guide users through session verification, account recovery, password reset, and security procedures.

COMMON ACCOUNT RECOVERY FAQ
==================================================

Q: How do I recover access to my account?
A: Use password recovery options through your registered email address or phone number.

Q: What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access?
A: Change your password immediately, enable two-factor authentication, and review login activity.

Q: How does two-factor authentication work?
A: Users must enter a verification code in addition to their password during login.

Q: Why is account security important?
A: Strong security measures help prevent unauthorized access, data theft, and account misuse.

Q: What is the best way to protect an Instagram account?
A: Use a strong password, enable two-factor authentication, avoid suspicious links, and regularly review login activity.
